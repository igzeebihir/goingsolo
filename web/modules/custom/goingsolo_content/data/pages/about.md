goingsolo.ie is a home for holidays in Ireland that are fair to people travelling alone.

Too many hotel deals are priced "per person sharing", and the solo traveller pays a single supplement that can add 50% or more to the bill. Some places do it differently. They have proper single rooms, solo packages, or simply charge one person a fair price. We find them and put them in one place, organised by county.

## How we check deals

Every deal links to the provider's own website and shows the date we last checked it. We add deals in three ways:

- We search hotel, B&B and tour operator websites for solo packages and single rates.
- We ring providers and ask what they offer people travelling alone.
- Providers tell us about their offers through the [List your solo offer](/list-your-offer) form.

We never invent prices. If a provider doesn't publish a solo price, we say so and link to their booking page. Prices change, so always confirm the solo rate when you book.

## Who it's for

Anyone holidaying alone in Ireland: people living here who want a break, visitors from abroad, widowed or divorced travellers, people whose partner doesn't like travel, and people who just prefer their own company.
