Each deal on goingsolo.ie shows a **Last checked** date. That is the day we confirmed the offer on the provider's website or by phone.

## What the labels mean

- **No single supplement**: one person pays a price set for one person, with no extra charge for being alone.
- **Room share, no supplement**: you pay the per-person price if you agree to share a room with another guest of the same gender. A private room usually costs extra.
- **Reduced solo rate**: there is a single occupancy price that is lower than paying for two.
- **Single supplement applies**: the provider charges extra for solo use, but we list it because the offer is still good value or especially welcoming to solo guests.

## Featured deals

Some providers pay to feature a deal. Featured deals show a **Featured** label and appear first in lists. Paying never changes what we say about an offer, and featured deals are checked in the same way as all the others.

## Links and commission

Some links may earn us a commission if you book. It never costs you more, and it helps keep the site free.
