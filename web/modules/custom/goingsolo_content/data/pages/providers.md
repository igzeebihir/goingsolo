goingsolo.ie brings you guests who are actively looking for a solo break in Ireland. Listing a genuine solo offer is free. If you want more visibility, there are paid options.

## Free listing

Tell us about your solo rate, single rooms or solo package using the [List your solo offer](/list-your-offer) form. We check it and add it to your county page and the map.

## Featured listing: €49 for 30 days

Your deal shows first on the deals page and your county page, with a Featured label and a highlighted marker on the map. [Feature a listing](/providers/featured-listing).

## Provider Pro: €29 a month

For hotels and operators with several solo offers. Feature up to three deals at a time, update your offers yourself and see how many people click through to your booking page. [Go Pro](/providers/pro).
