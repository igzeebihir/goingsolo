This page explains what personal data goingsolo.ie collects and why. It is a starting point and should be reviewed before the site launches.

## What we collect

- **Account details** if you register or join the Solo Club: your name, email address and the counties you want alerts for.
- **Payment details** are handled by our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.
- **Messages** you send through our contact forms.
- **Usage data** such as pages visited, collected with cookies when you agree to them.

## Advertising

We show ads from Google AdSense to visitors who are not Solo Club members. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Visitors in the EEA and UK are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time. You can learn more at [Google's advertising policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Contact us using the [contact form](/contact). You can also complain to the [Data Protection Commission](https://www.dataprotection.ie).
