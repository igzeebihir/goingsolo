Eating alone is the part of a solo trip people worry about most. The Solo-friendly dining badge marks hotels, restaurants and cafés where a table for one is welcome, not tolerated.

## What we look for

To earn the badge, a venue has to meet at least four of these:

- **Good seats for one.** Counter or bar seating, window tables or a communal table, not the table by the kitchen door.
- **No minimum spend for two.** Set menus, tasting menus and early-bird offers can be ordered by one person.
- **A welcome, not a question.** Staff do not ask "just one?" or rush you through.
- **Something to read.** Papers, books or magazines, or Wi-Fi that works.
- **Smaller portions or half bottles.** Wine by the glass and half bottles, and dishes that are not only for sharing.
- **Dining with others if you want it.** A chef's table, supper club or shared table on some nights.

## How it works

1. Tell us about your venue using the [List your solo offer](/list-your-offer) form and say you would like the badge.
2. We check with recent solo diners and, where we can, visit.
3. Venues that qualify get the badge on their goingsolo.ie listing and a badge to show on their own website and door.

The badge is free while we build up the list. We check it again every year, and we will remove it if solo travellers tell us the welcome has changed.
