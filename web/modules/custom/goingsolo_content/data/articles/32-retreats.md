Retreats are one of the few kinds of holiday where coming alone is the norm. Most guests arrive on their own, meals are shared, and the schedule gives your days a gentle shape.

## Types of retreat

- **Yoga retreats**, from beginner-friendly weekends to week-long intensives.
- **Walking and wellness retreats** combining hikes, yoga and good food.
- **Silent and meditation retreats**, sometimes in religious or Buddhist centres.
- **Women's retreats**, often combining wellness, walking and sea swimming.
- **Creative retreats** for writing, painting or photography.

## What to look for

- **Room arrangements.** Many retreats have shared rooms. Ask whether private rooms are available and what they cost.
- **Group size.** Small groups of 8 to 15 feel more personal.
- **What's included.** Most include meals and classes. Check whether transfers, treatments and activities cost extra.
- **The teachers.** Look for qualified, experienced teachers and recent reviews.
- **Location and access.** Some retreats are deep in the countryside. Ask about pickups from the nearest train or bus station.

## When to go

Retreats run all year. Spring and autumn retreats are often cheaper than summer, and a winter retreat with fires and sea swims can be wonderful.
