North Clare combines the Cliffs of Moher, the strange limestone landscape of the Burren, and some of Ireland's best traditional music. It works well as a solo trip from Galway or Ennis.

## The Cliffs of Moher

The cliffs rise more than 200 metres above the Atlantic. The visitor centre gets very busy in the middle of the day, so go early or late. For a quieter experience, walk part of the **Cliffs of Moher Coastal Walk** from Doolin, or join a boat trip from Doolin pier in calm weather to see the cliffs from below.

## The Burren

The Burren is a vast area of bare limestone pavement, home to an unusual mix of Arctic, Alpine and Mediterranean plants that flower in late spring. Highlights include:

- **Poulnabrone portal tomb**, a dolmen over 5,000 years old.
- **Burren National Park**, with waymarked walking loops.
- **The Burren Perfumery** and small craft producers.
- **Aillwee Cave**, a show cave with guided tours.

Guided walks with local experts are a great way to understand the landscape and meet other walkers.

## Music

Doolin is famous for traditional music, and sessions run in its pubs most nights. Ennistymon and Lisdoonvarna also have good music and fewer crowds.

## Getting there without a car

Buses run from Galway and Ennis to Doolin and the Cliffs, and many day tours from Galway cover the cliffs and the Burren together. To explore more deeply, stay in Doolin, Lisdoonvarna or Ballyvaughan and join guided walks.
