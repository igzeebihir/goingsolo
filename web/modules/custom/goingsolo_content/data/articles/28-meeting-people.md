One of the joys of travelling solo is choosing when to be alone and when to have company. Ireland makes the second part easy. People are curious and chatty, and there are lots of ways to join in.

## Traditional music sessions

Sit near the musicians at a session, buy a drink and listen. Between tunes, people around you will often strike up a conversation. Sessions are usually informal and free.

## Walking festivals and clubs

Ireland has many walking festivals, from Achill to the Mournes, with guided walks for all levels. They're a lovely way to meet other walkers. Local hillwalking clubs sometimes welcome visitors on their weekend walks.

## Small-group tours

Day tours and multi-day small-group tours bring together people from all over the world. Several operators run trips aimed at solo travellers, and some match you with a roommate to avoid the single supplement.

## Classes and workshops

Try a cookery class, a pottery or craft workshop, a set dancing class or a Gaelic games experience. You'll learn something and meet people in a relaxed setting.

## Hostels and communal tables

Even if you're not on a tight budget, a private room in a good hostel gives you a common room and kitchen where it's easy to meet other travellers. Some hotels and guesthouses have communal breakfast tables.

## Parkrun

Free, timed 5km runs or walks take place every Saturday morning in parks across Ireland. Register online in advance, then just turn up. Everyone's welcome, and there's usually coffee afterwards.

## Volunteering

Conservation and heritage groups sometimes welcome volunteers for a day. It's a meaningful way to connect with a place.
