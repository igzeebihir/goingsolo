County Wicklow, the "Garden of Ireland", is Dublin's mountain playground. For solo travellers it's ideal: easy to reach, with well-marked trails and a regular private bus service to its most famous site.

## Glendalough

The monastic site founded by St Kevin sits in a glacial valley with two lakes. The round tower and churches are free to visit. Several waymarked trails start from the Upper Lake car park and visitor centre, from the gentle Green Road to the Spinc loop, which climbs above the Upper Lake on boardwalks and gives superb views.

The St Kevin's Bus runs daily from Dublin city to Glendalough via Bray, so you don't need a car.

## The Wicklow Way

Ireland's oldest waymarked trail runs for about 130km from Marlay Park in Dublin to Clonegal in Carlow. Walking companies offer self-guided trips with luggage transfer between B&Bs, which is a good way to walk it alone with support.

## Powerscourt

Powerscourt Estate near Enniskerry has formal gardens and a waterfall, which is reached by a separate road.

## Coastal Wicklow

The cliff walk from Bray to Greystones is one of the best short walks on the east coast, and both ends are on the DART line.

## Safety

Mountain weather in Wicklow can change quickly, and mist on high ground is common. Stay on marked trails unless you're an experienced hill walker, tell someone your route and carry a charged phone.
