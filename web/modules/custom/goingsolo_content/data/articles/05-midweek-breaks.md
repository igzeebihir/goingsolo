If you have any flexibility, a midweek break is one of the simplest ways to make solo travel in Ireland better value.

## Lower prices

Leisure hotels fill up at weekends with couples and groups. On a Monday or Tuesday night many have empty rooms, and they would rather sell one at a lower rate than leave it empty. That's when single occupancy discounts and solo packages are easiest to get. Many "midweek special" packages run Sunday to Thursday.

## More attention

A quieter hotel means staff have more time. Solo guests often find it easier to chat to the bar staff, get advice on walks and get a good table at dinner without a booking.

## Quieter sights

Popular places like Glendalough, the Cliffs of Moher, Kylemore Abbey and the Rock of Cashel are much calmer midweek, especially outside summer. Early morning visits on a weekday can feel almost private.

## What to watch for

- **Business hotels in cities** work the other way round. They can be busier and dearer midweek and cheaper at weekends.
- **Restaurants in small towns** sometimes close on Mondays and Tuesdays, especially in winter. Check before you go, or choose a hotel with its own restaurant.
- **Tours and ferries** to islands may run less often outside summer. Check timetables.
