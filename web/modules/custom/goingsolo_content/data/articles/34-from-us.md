Ireland is one of the most popular solo destinations for Americans: it's English-speaking, welcoming and easy to get around, and many visitors have family roots to explore. Here's what to know.

## Getting there

Direct flights connect many US cities with Dublin and Shannon. US travellers can clear US immigration at Dublin and Shannon airports before flying home (US preclearance), which saves time on arrival back in the States.

US citizens don't need a visa for short visits to the Republic of Ireland. Check the latest entry requirements before you travel, including for Northern Ireland, which is part of the UK and may require an electronic travel authorisation.

## Money

The Republic uses the euro, and Northern Ireland uses sterling. Cards are widely accepted, including contactless. Use a card without foreign transaction fees and carry a little cash for small shops and rural areas. Tipping in restaurants is usually around 10% to 15% for good service; it's not expected for drinks at the bar.

## Pace yourself

The most common mistake is trying to see the whole island in a week. Distances look small on the map but rural roads are slow. Pick two or three bases, such as Dublin, Galway and Killarney, and do day trips from each.

## Driving

Driving is on the left, many roads are narrow, and car hire insurance can be expensive. Read our guide to [driving alone in Ireland](/guides/driving-alone-in-ireland-rentals-rural-roads-and-tips), or consider trains, buses and tours instead.

Some US travel companies sell Ireland packages priced specifically for solo travellers, with no single supplement. These often bundle flights, a car and B&Bs. Check our [deals page](/deals) for current examples.

## Tracing your roots

If you have Irish ancestry, the EPIC museum in Dublin, the National Library's genealogy service and county heritage centres can help. Bring any names, places and dates you have.

## Jet lag

Most flights from the US arrive early in the morning. Plan a gentle first day, get outside in daylight, and resist a long nap.
