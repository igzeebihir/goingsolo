Connemara, in the west of County Galway, is one of the most striking landscapes in Ireland: the Twelve Bens mountains, lakes, bog and a coastline of small harbours and white-sand beaches.

## With a car

Driving gives you the most freedom. A popular loop runs from Galway through Oughterard and Maam Cross to Recess, Roundstone and Clifden, then north to Letterfrack and Kylemore Abbey before returning via Leenane and Maam. Roads are narrow in places, so take your time.

## Without a car

You can still see a lot. Day tours from Galway cover Kylemore Abbey and the main scenic routes. Buses run from Galway to Clifden, and Clifden is a good base with shops, pubs and restaurants in walking distance.

## Highlights

- **Kylemore Abbey** and its Victorian walled garden.
- **Connemara National Park** at Letterfrack, with marked trails up Diamond Hill. The views over the coast and islands are superb.
- **Roundstone**, a fishing village with nearby Gurteen and Dog's Bay beaches.
- **The Sky Road** loop from Clifden, which can be walked or cycled.
- **Inishbofin**, an island reached by ferry from Cleggan, with easy loop walks.

## Where to stay

Clifden has hotels and guesthouses in town. For something special, Ballynahinch Castle near Recess has solo packages with hotel-arranged walks and tours. See our [Galway deals](/county/galway).

## Weather

Connemara weather changes fast. Bring a waterproof jacket and layers even in summer, and check the forecast before climbing.
