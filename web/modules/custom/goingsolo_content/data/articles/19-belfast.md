Belfast has become one of the most rewarding cities on the island to visit alone. It's compact, good value, full of friendly people and has a lot to say about its past. The Enterprise train links it with Dublin in a little over two hours.

Northern Ireland uses sterling, not euro, so bring a card that works without foreign transaction fees.

## In Belfast

- **Titanic Belfast**, on the site where the ship was built, is the city's most popular attraction. Book ahead.
- **Black taxi tours** of the murals and peace walls, led by local drivers, give a personal view of the Troubles. They're well suited to solo visitors.
- **Belfast City Hall** offers free guided tours.
- **St George's Market** at weekends, for food, crafts and live music.
- **The Ulster Museum** and the Botanic Gardens in the university quarter, both free.

The Cathedral Quarter has many of the city's best pubs and restaurants.

## The Causeway Coast

The coast road from Belfast to Derry is one of the most beautiful drives in Ireland, past the Glens of Antrim, Carrick-a-Rede rope bridge and the **Giant's Causeway**. Day tours from Belfast cover the highlights, which saves driving. Trains and buses also reach Portrush and Coleraine, and the Causeway Rambler bus runs along the coast in summer.

## Derry

The walled city of Derry is a short trip further on. Walking the 17th-century walls is free, and the city's museums tell the story of Bloody Sunday and the civil rights movement.

Belfast has many modern hotels with reasonable single rates, especially on weeknights. See current [Northern Ireland deals](/county/northern-ireland).
