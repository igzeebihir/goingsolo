West Cork is where many Irish people go to unwind. It's a region of colourful small towns, quiet peninsulas, islands and some of the best food in the country. It suits solo travellers who want to slow down.

## Choosing a base

- **Kinsale** is the gateway, with a pretty harbour, a strong restaurant scene and Charles Fort. It's about 40 minutes from Cork city by bus.
- **Clonakilty** is a lively market town close to Inchydoney beach.
- **Skibbereen** is a good hub for Lough Hyne, Baltimore and the islands, and has a busy Saturday market.
- **Bantry** sits at the head of Bantry Bay, between the Sheep's Head and Beara peninsulas, with Bantry House on the edge of town.

## Islands

Ferries from Baltimore serve **Sherkin Island** and **Cape Clear**, Ireland's most southerly inhabited island. Garinish (Ilnacullin), with its Italian gardens, is a short boat ride from Glengarriff. All make a calm day out on your own.

## Walking

The **Sheep's Head Way** is a waymarked trail with sections that make good day walks, and the peninsula is quiet even in summer. The Beara Way is longer and wilder.

## Getting around

Buses link Cork city with Kinsale, Clonakilty, Skibbereen and Bantry, but services to smaller villages and peninsulas are limited. If you want to explore widely, a car makes it easier. Otherwise pick one town, and use local taxis, walking and boat trips.
