Walking alone is one of the great pleasures of a solo trip, and walking with a dog is even better. These routes are easy to follow and close to places that take dogs.

## Greenways

Ireland's greenways are flat, traffic-free and well signed, which makes them ideal for a walk with a dog. Keep your dog on a short lead because cyclists come up fast.

- **Great Western Greenway, Mayo.** From Westport towards Newport and Achill, with views of Clew Bay.
- **Waterford Greenway.** From Waterford city to Dungarvan along an old railway, with viaducts and a tunnel. Kilmacthomas is a good halfway stop.
- **Old Rail Trail, Westmeath.** From Athlone towards Mullingar, close to Lough Ree.

## Long beaches

Out of season, many Irish beaches are almost empty. In summer, check the signs, because some councils restrict dogs on bathing beaches during the day.

- **Enniscrone, Sligo.** Miles of sand on Killala Bay.
- **Inchydoney, Cork.** Near Clonakilty, with dunes at each end.
- **Killahoey, Donegal.** Beside Dunfanaghy, with Horn Head above it.

## The Burren

The green roads of the Burren in Clare are old stone-walled lanes over the limestone, quiet and easy to follow. Livestock graze here, so keep your dog on a lead.

## Before you go

- Carry water, bags and a lead even if your dog is usually off-lead.
- Some walks cross farmland where dogs are not allowed. If a sign says no dogs, turn back.
- Tell someone your route, as you would on any solo walk.
