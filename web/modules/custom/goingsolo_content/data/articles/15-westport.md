Westport is one of the best small towns in Ireland for solo travellers. It's attractive, compact and full of pubs and restaurants, and it has a direct train from Dublin Heuston. The Great Western Greenway starts at the edge of town.

## The Great Western Greenway

This traffic-free trail follows an old railway line from Westport to Newport, Mulranny and Achill, about 42km in all. Bike hire companies in Westport rent bikes, including e-bikes, and can collect you at the far end so you only ride one way. The Westport to Newport section is a good half-day; Newport to Mulranny has wonderful views over Clew Bay.

## Croagh Patrick

Ireland's holy mountain rises above Clew Bay. The climb takes most people three to four hours return and the upper section is steep loose scree, so wear proper boots and check the weather. If you'd rather not climb, the visitor centre at the base and the views from Murrisk are lovely.

## Westport House

The historic house and estate at the edge of town has gardens and walks.

## Achill Island

Reached by road bridge, Achill has some of Ireland's finest beaches, including Keem Bay, and the Deserted Village at Slievemore. Buses run from Westport to Achill, but it's easier by car or bike.

## Evenings

Westport has a strong music scene. Matt Molloy's on Bridge Street, owned by the Chieftains' flute player, is famous for sessions.

## Where to stay

Westport has hotels, guesthouses and B&Bs for most budgets. Book early for summer weekends. See current [Mayo deals](/county/mayo).
