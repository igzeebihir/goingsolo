title: 'The cheapest times of year to holiday alone in Ireland'
topic: 'Planning and money'
standfirst: 'When hotels are quiet, solo rates are easier to find. Here is how the Irish year breaks down.'
---
Hotel prices in Ireland swing a long way between seasons. For a solo traveller that matters twice over: when hotels are busy, they're less willing to give one person a fair rate. Pick a quiet time and you'll find more solo deals, more single rooms and more flexibility.

## The quietest months: November to March (excluding Christmas)

From mid-November to mid-March, outside the Christmas and New Year period, Irish hotels are at their quietest. This is when solo packages and midweek specials are most common. The weather is mixed and the days are short, but cities like Dublin, Galway, Cork and Kilkenny have plenty to do indoors, and a cosy country house hotel with a fire is at its best.

Watch out for: rugby international weekends in Dublin, St Patrick's week in March, and big concerts, all of which push city prices up.

## The shoulder seasons: April to May, and September to October

Spring and early autumn are the best balance of price and weather. Gardens are in bloom in May, the evenings are long, and walking conditions are good. Schools are back in September, so family demand drops and coastal hotels have more space.

Watch out for: Easter, the May and October bank holiday weekends, and mid-term breaks in late October and mid-February.

## The busiest time: June to August

Summer is peak season along the Wild Atlantic Way, in Kerry and in Dublin. Solo deals are hardest to find, and some hotels require minimum stays at weekends. If you do travel in summer, aim for Sunday to Thursday nights, look at towns slightly away from the main tourist routes, and book early.

## Days of the week matter too

Across the year, Sunday to Thursday is cheaper than Friday and Saturday in most leisure hotels. City business hotels can work the other way: they're busier midweek and cheaper at weekends.

## A quick rule of thumb

If you can, travel midweek, outside school holidays, between September and May. You'll pay less, find more solo offers and have more of Ireland to yourself.
