A small-group tour is a great way to see Ireland if you're travelling alone. Someone else handles the driving, the hotels and the logistics, and you have company for as much or as little of the day as you like.

## Questions to ask

**What's the single supplement?** This varies hugely. Some companies charge nothing for solo travellers, some charge a modest amount, and some pass on the full cost of a double room. Some tours are designed specifically for solo travellers, with your own room included.

**Is there a room-share option?** Some operators will pair you with a same-gender guest so you pay the sharing price. Ask what happens if they can't find a match: some then give you a private room at no extra cost.

**How big is the group?** "Small group" can mean 12 people or 40. Smaller groups feel more personal and reach places big coaches can't.

**What's the pace?** Look at how many hotel changes there are and how much time is spent on the coach each day. Fewer hotel moves usually means a more relaxed trip.

**Who are the other travellers?** Some tours attract younger backpackers, others mainly travellers over 50. Read reviews to get a feel for the group.

**What's included?** Compare meals, entrance fees, activities and tips, not just the headline price.

## Types of tour

- **Escorted coach tours** with a driver-guide, usually staying in hotels.
- **Active tours** for walking, cycling or kayaking, often in smaller groups with rural accommodation.
- **Solo-only tours**, where everyone is travelling alone and gets their own room.
- **Day tours** from a city base, if you'd rather sleep in the same place each night.
