title: 'Getting around Ireland without a car'
topic: 'Getting around'
standfirst: 'Trains, buses, greenways and day tours can take you almost everywhere a solo traveller needs to go.'
---
Renting a car alone is expensive, and driving Ireland's narrow roads isn't everyone's idea of a holiday. The good news is that you can see a great deal of the country without one.

## Trains

Irish Rail (Iarnród Éireann) runs intercity trains from Dublin to Cork, Galway, Limerick, Killarney, Tralee, Westport, Sligo, Waterford, Kilkenny, Wexford and Rosslare. In Northern Ireland, NI Railways runs trains from Belfast to Derry and along the coast, and the Enterprise links Belfast with Dublin.

Book online in advance for the cheapest fares, and reserve a seat on busy routes.

## Buses

Bus Éireann and private operators such as Citylink, GoBus and Dublin Coach run express coaches between cities and airports, often with Wi-Fi and charging points. Local buses reach smaller towns, but rural services can be infrequent, especially on Sundays and in winter. TFI Local Link services fill some gaps in rural areas.

Use the **TFI Journey Planner** app to plan trips in the Republic, and **Translink** in Northern Ireland.

## The Leap Card

In Dublin, Cork, Galway, Limerick and Waterford, a TFI Leap Card gives cheaper fares on city buses, the Luas and the DART than paying cash. Visitors can buy a Leap Visitor Card at Dublin Airport.

## Day tours

From Dublin, Galway, Cork, Killarney and Belfast, coach day tours reach places like the Cliffs of Moher, Connemara, the Ring of Kerry and the Giant's Causeway. For one person, a tour is usually much cheaper than renting a car for the day, and you get company and commentary.

## Bikes and greenways

Greenways like the Waterford Greenway and the Great Western Greenway are flat, traffic-free and have bike hire with shuttle services, so you can ride one way.

## Free travel for over-66s

Residents of Ireland aged 66 and over can travel free on most public transport with a Free Travel Pass.

## Taxis

App-based taxis are widely available in cities. In rural areas, ask your accommodation for a local taxi number and book ahead.
